argued: Makes a claim about a game or industry topic and backs it with at least one concrete reason: specific gameplay details, comparisons, stats, sources, or personal experience explained in enough detail to show why.
hot_take: States a confident opinion or verdict with no reason given, or only a vague one. Naming specific games does not count as support. 
reaction: Expresses an emotion or personal feeling without making a claim others could agree or disagree with.
question: Primary purpose is to ask for information, help, opinions, or recommendations. Rhetorical questions that really make a point are labeled by the point instead.
